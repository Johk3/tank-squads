Version: 0.15.1
Date: 2026-09-24
  Changes:
    - Offensive divisions of a single tank kind, such as carriers alone, now gather on the staging arc outside a nest before they attack, instead of driving straight at it. Carriers attack together once gathered.
    - The mobile headquarters flattens trees, rocks and cliffs in its way instead of getting stuck in forests. It never destroys buildings.
    - The mobile headquarters takes 60 seconds to build instead of 10 minutes and costs a fifth as much: 400 steel, 100 gears, 40 electric engines, 40 processing units, 40 solar panels, 40 accumulators, 4 roboports and 4 radars.
  Bugfixes:
    - The three chaingun carrier training recipes show their names instead of an unknown key.
    - The mobile headquarters' radar no longer freezes the game and hides your other divisions in fog of war. It now keeps 4 chunks around it live and scans out to 16, a little more than a base radar, instead of 8 and 32.

Version: 0.15.0
Date: 2026-09-24
  Features:
    - Injured escort and scout soldiers now retreat to a mobile headquarters when it is nearer than any barracks. They stop within its 24-tile healing reach and follow it when it drives on.
  Changes:
    - A patrolling headquarters no longer sets the pace for its division. It drives the innermost lane, near the middle of the route, and leads only a division of headquarters.
    - Starting an escort no longer stops the headquarters; it keeps its last order, as in scouting. A division with only a headquarters cannot escort.
    - A headquarters no longer counts towards a barracks' reinforcement target, which counts carriers. A barracks that trains headquarters still counts them, so it stops at its target.

Version: 0.14.0

    - Mobile headquarters: a huge, very slow and unarmed command vehicle, built at a barracks after its own research. It has 400 HP and heals soldiers within 24 tiles, also on the move.

Version: 0.13.0
Date: 2026-09-24
  Features:
    - Scouting divisions split into up to four teams, one per three soldiers, that each explore their own direction. Bigger divisions make bigger teams, and every team keeps a flame tank or carrier in front of its siege tanks.
    - Scout teams move in formation: flame tanks lead, carriers follow in a V, and siege tanks trail 30 tiles back, still in range of the front. Teams advance in 32-tile hops and wait for every tank before the next hop.
    - Badly damaged scouts drive back to a barracks to heal in guarded convoys, and a team below half its health or down to half its tanks pulls back together.
    - Scout teams keep pushing outward past charted land. A team blocked by water or cliffs, or left with only siege tanks, joins the nearest team, and scouting stops only when every direction is blocked.
  Changes:
    - The retreat health, rejoin health and barracks search range settings now apply to scouts as well as escorts.
